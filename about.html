Our point of view

Useful beats impressive.

We started Auralis because the hard part of AI is rarely the model. It is the context around the model: the source, the standard, the handoff, the moment when someone needs to trust the result.

Our job is to make that context easier to see and easier to improve. The best systems feel less like magic and more like a well-run team.

What we believe

Principles for building with care.

Make the invisible visible

People can improve what they can inspect. We surface the trace, the source, and the decision.

Respect judgment

Automation should create room for better human decisions, not pretend they are unnecessary.

Grow from the edge

The unusual case teaches you more than the happy path. We design for learning in public.

A short history

From a working session to a company.

2022

The first workflow

Three of us built a small tool to help a support team understand why their AI assistant failed on the cases that mattered most.

2023

Quality became the product

Teams asked for better evaluation, better sources, and a way to bring domain experts into the loop. Auralis took shape around that need.

2024

The system widened

Product, operations, and research teams began using the same foundation to build workflows beyond support.

Today

Still close to the work

We are a small, distributed team focused on making dependable AI feel like a normal part of how good work gets done.
